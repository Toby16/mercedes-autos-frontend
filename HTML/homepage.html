Mercedes Automotives is an automobile dealership based in Deutchland, Germany. The company deals exclusively in brand new passenger, commercial, E-classed, and S-classed explicit vehicles.
Its stable of brands comprises all classes of Mercedes-Benz.

Our journey began in March 1886 when our Founder and Chairman, Karl Benz, registered the company with a mission to transform the undeserved European automobile industry.
Over fourteen decades later, Mercedes Automotives has surpassed its initial aspirations and the company continues to set the pace in the evolution of the country's automobile sector. We are proud partners of leading multinationals and several public sector institutions.
Today, Mercedes Automotives is the largest dealer in the Mercedes-Benz segment.

The Mercedes-Benz brand continues to set the benchmark globally in luxury vehicle segment. Mercedes Automtives enjoys a sterling relationship with Daimler AG (manufacturers of Mercedes-Benz vehicles) and has been a catalyst for the growth of the brand.
